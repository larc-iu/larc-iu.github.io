Title: Luke Gessler named a CORE Institute Fellow on Indigenous language revitalization
Date: 2026-10-09

Congratulations to LARC director Luke Gessler, who has been selected for the [CORE Institute Fellows Program](https://www.core-institute.org/)
on Indigenous Language Revitalization! The fellowship brings together a small cohort to shape what
responsible, community-centered AI looks like in Indigenous language revitalization, grounded in
community priorities and data sovereignty.
