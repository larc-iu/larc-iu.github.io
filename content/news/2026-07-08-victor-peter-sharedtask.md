Title: LARC at ACL 2026: 🏆 Victor and Peter take #1 on AmericasNLP 2026 shared task for Guaraní
Date: 2026-07-08

Congratulations to IUCL PhD students Phakphum Artkaew and Victor Shi on their submission
of the [IUHoosiers system](https://aclanthology.org/2026.americasnlp-6.21/) to the 
[AmericasNLP 2026 shared task](https://americasnlp.org/2026_st.html) for culturally-sensitive
image caption generation, which [**placed first out of 7 systems**](https://aclanthology.org/2026.americasnlp-6.27/) 
for the Guaraní track! 

AmericasNLP 2026 was held at ACL 2026 in San Diego, where faculty member Luke Gessler also
presented [two](https://aclanthology.org/2026.computel-1.14/) [works](https://aclanthology.org/2026.computel-1.15/)
at [the ComputEL workshop](https://computel-workshop.org/computel-9/) and co-organized [LAW-XX](https://sigann.github.io/LAW-XX-2026/)
