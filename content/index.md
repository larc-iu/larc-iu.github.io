Summary: The Linguistic Algorithms, Resources, and Conservation group at Indiana University Bloomington.

We are the **L**inguistic **A**lgorithms, **R**esources, and **C**onservation group at Indiana University Bloomington's [Department of Linguistics](https://linguistics.indiana.edu/), led by [Prof. Luke Gessler](https://lgessler.com).
We are united by our interest in algorithms and resources for computationally processing languages which are 
endangered or "low-resource" (lacking large quantities of digitized data).

Our recent work has attempted to advance the following goals:

* Realizing the benefits of human language technologies in endangered language documentation and conservation
* Developing higher-quality language technologies for low-resource languages
* Using techniques and knowledge from linguistics to develop evaluations for low-resource language technologies
