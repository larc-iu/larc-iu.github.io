Example news post

August 14, 2026

This file is a draft, so it does not appear on the news page or the home page. It exists as a starting point: copy it to content/news/your-post-name.md, edit the metadata above, and delete the Status: draft line when it is ready to publish.

A few notes on the metadata:

  • Title and Date are required. The date controls ordering, and whether the post is recent enough to appear on the home page.
  • Summary is optional. If you leave it out, the news page shows the opening of the post instead. Writing one explicitly usually reads better.

The body is ordinary Markdown, so you can use links, bold, lists, and images as needed. Posts are meant to be short: a paragraph or two announcing a talk, a paper, an award, or a new group member.
