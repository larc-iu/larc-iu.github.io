Projects

Software, datasets, and resources built in the group. Most come with a paper describing the work in more detail; see Publications for the full list.

PrOnto active

Language model evaluation data for 859 languages, built by projecting annotations onto Bible translations. Makes it possible to benchmark multilingual models on languages that have no purpose-built test set.

Luke Gessler

Paper

MicroBERT maintained

Small monolingual BERT models for low-resource languages, trained with parameter reduction and multitask learning. Shows that useful pretrained models are reachable well below the data scale usually assumed.

Luke Gessler, Amir Zeldes

Paper

Midas Loop maintained

A human-in-the-loop annotation system for large multilayer corpora, which prioritises the annotations where human attention pays off most.

Luke Gessler, Lauren Levine, Amir Zeldes

Paper

Xposition maintained

An online multilingual database of adpositional semantics, documenting how prepositions and postpositions carry meaning across languages.

Luke Gessler, Austin Blodgett, Joseph C. Ledford, Nathan Schneider

Paper
