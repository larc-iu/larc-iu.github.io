LARC at ACL 2026: 🏆 Victor and Peter take #1 on AmericasNLP 2026 shared task for Guaraní

July 08, 2026

Congratulations to IUCL PhD students Phakphum Artkaew and Victor Shi on their submission of the IUHoosiers system to the AmericasNLP 2026 shared task for culturally-sensitive image caption generation, which placed first out of 7 systems for the Guaraní track!

AmericasNLP 2026 was held at ACL 2026 in San Diego, where faculty member Luke Gessler also presented two works at the ComputEL workshop and co-organized LAW-XX
