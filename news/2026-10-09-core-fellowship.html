Luke Gessler named a CORE Institute Fellow on Indigenous language revitalization

October 09, 2026

Congratulations to LARC director Luke Gessler, who has been selected for the CORE Institute Fellows Program on Indigenous Language Revitalization! The fellowship brings together a small cohort to shape what responsible, community-centered AI looks like in Indigenous language revitalization, grounded in community priorities and data sovereignty.
